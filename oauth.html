How Roblox OAuth 2.0 Verification Works

Official, passwordless account linking between Roblox and Discord

  • What is Roblox OAuth 2.0? OAuth 2.0 is Roblox’s official authorization standard. Instead of typing your password into a third-party site, you sign in directly on apis.roblox.com and approve exactly what information may be shared.
  • Minimal Scopes Only: This application requests only openid and profile. That provides your Roblox User ID, username, and display name—nothing else.
  • What Is Never Accessed: Your Roblox password, email address, 2FA codes, Robux balance, inventory, and friend list are never requested, visible, or stored.
  • How the Link Is Secured: When you click Verify yourself in Discord, the bot generates a short-lived (15-minute), HMAC-SHA256 signed state token tied to your Discord User ID so no one else can claim or forge your verification.
  • After You Approve: Roblox redirects back to jsontext.me/callback, the Worker reads your public Roblox profile once in memory, grants your verified Discord role, and logs the Discord-to-Roblox ID link for server administrators.
