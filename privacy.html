Privacy Policy

Applies to jsontext.me and the jsontext Discord Bot (Roblox OAuth 2.0 & Community Services)

  • Scope & Purpose: This policy explains how jsontext.me and the jsontext Discord bot collect, use, and protect limited user information solely to provide Roblox account verification, Discord role assignment, server moderation logging, and support ticket management.
  • Information Collected via Roblox OAuth 2.0: During verification, we request only the openid and profile scopes from Roblox. We receive your Roblox User ID (sub), username (preferred_username), and display name (name) together with your Discord User ID.
  • Information Collected in Discord: Within configured server channels, the bot processes moderation audit events, message edit/delete logs, support ticket channel metadata, and linked Discord-to-Roblox user IDs for administrative lookup.
  • Information We Do Not Collect: We never request, access, or store Roblox or Discord passwords, email addresses, billing or Robux information, friend lists, or private direct messages.
  • Token & Credential Handling: Verification links use short-lived (15-minute), HMAC-SHA256 signed state tokens to prevent forgery. OAuth 2.0 authorization codes and access tokens are used once in memory to retrieve your Roblox profile and are never persisted.
  • Data Sharing & Third Parties: We do not sell, rent, or use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics. Data is transmitted over HTTPS only to the official Discord API and Roblox OAuth 2.0 API as required to operate the service.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Verification links and server logs are retained only as long as necessary for server administration and security. You may request unlinking or deletion of your stored verification record or ticket data at any time by opening a support ticket or contacting a server administrator in Discord.
